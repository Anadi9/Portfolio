Twenty automations, grouped by what they actually fix. Each one lists the time
it saves, the tool to build it in, and the exact trigger → action chain, so
you're not guessing what to connect to what.

None of these need code, just a Zapier or n8n account and 10–20 minutes of setup
each. Five of them have a full build guide with copyable n8n JSON:
[the deep-dive drop](/drops/automate).

> **How to use this:** pick **one** automation that fixes your biggest weekly
> annoyance. Set it up. Let it run for a week. Then come back for the next one.
> Trying to build all twenty at once is how people quit. One at a time is how
> people finish.

## Content and growth engine

*Automations that keep the content flywheel turning without you babysitting it.*

### 1. Comment-to-DM lead funnel + auto-log

**Saves ~3 hrs/week · Easy · Instagram Automations (native) + Zapier or n8n**

Someone comments your keyword, they're auto-DM'd your resource, and their
username, keyword and timestamp get logged, so you have a running list of
everyone who asked without scrolling your comments to find them.

**Trigger → action:** new comment matches keyword → Instagram auto-DM (native,
free) → Zapier *New IG mention* trigger → add row to Google Sheets or Airtable.

**Setup:** the DM part needs no extra tool at all: turn on Professional
Dashboard → Automations → Keyword replies. Layer Zapier on only for the logging.
→ [Full build guide with the n8n JSON](/drops/automate)

### 2. One-post-everywhere repost

**Saves ~3 hrs/week · Easy · Repurpose.io or Buffer, triggered by Zapier**

Post a Reel once and it automatically reposts to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and X
the same day, with no manually re-uploading the same file four times.

**Trigger → action:** new IG post published → Repurpose.io or Buffer pulls the
file → auto-publishes or queues it across your other platforms.

**Setup:** connect all four accounts once, set your default caption and hashtag
pattern per platform, turn on auto-publish. ~15 minutes, one time.

### 3. Transcript → captions + hashtags

**Saves ~2 hrs/week · Medium · n8n + Claude or GPT API**

Drop a new video into a watched folder and it generates 3 caption options and 12
hashtags before you've opened your notes app.

**Trigger → action:** new file in a watched Drive/Dropbox folder → extract
transcript → send to Claude/GPT with a fixed prompt → post results to a Notion
row to pick from.

**Setup:** keep the prompt template fixed and feed it 5–10 of your best past
captions once as style examples, so the output already sounds like you.
→ [Full build guide with the n8n JSON](/drops/automate)

### 4. Auto-surface your best comment weekly

**Saves ~1 hr/week · Medium · n8n + Instagram API**

Every Sunday it scans the week's comments across your posts and surfaces the
single most-liked or most-thoughtful one, ready to reply to and reshare as a
story, instead of scrolling threads hunting for it.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled weekly → pull comments via IG API → sort by like
count → send the top one to a Slack or email alert.

**Setup:** start with "sort by likes" as the filter. Add an AI sentiment step
later if you want *most thoughtful* rather than just *most liked*.

### 5. Competitor swipe-file auto-capture

**Saves ~1.5 hrs/week · Easy · iOS Shortcuts or Zapier + Notion**

See a post worth stealing the idea from? Share it to a Shortcut, or to a
specific email or Slack channel, and it's logged into a Notion swipe-file
database with the link, a screenshot and a blank note field, instead of living
forever in your camera roll.

**Trigger → action:** share-sheet action or forwarded link → Zapier parses the
URL → creates a Notion row with link and thumbnail.

**Setup:** build one Notion database with columns for Link, Platform, Why It
Worked, and Status, then point the Shortcut at it. ~15 minutes.

## Leads, clients and money

*Automations that stop revenue leaking out through manual follow-up.*

### 6. Signed contract → onboarding packet

**Saves ~45 min per client · Medium · Zapier + PandaDoc/DocuSign + Notion**

The moment a contract is signed, the client gets a welcome email, a shared
onboarding doc, and a kickoff-call booking link, with no manual assembly per client.

**Trigger → action:** contract marked *signed* → Zapier duplicates your
onboarding Notion template → sends the welcome email with that link and a
Calendly link.

**Setup:** build the onboarding template once as a duplicable Notion page, then
point Zapier's *duplicate page* action at it per new signature.

### 7. Escalating unpaid-invoice reminders

**Saves ~1 hr/week, and gets you paid faster · Easy · Stripe or QuickBooks + Zapier**

An invoice sends the moment a deal closes, then reminders fire automatically on
a schedule if it isn't paid, with no awkward manual "just checking in" emails.

**Trigger → action:** CRM status → *Won* → create and send invoice → wait 5 days
→ check payment status → auto-send reminder if unpaid.

**Setup:** the trigger → delay → reminder pattern works with any invoicing tool
that has a Zapier or n8n integration, not just Stripe.
→ [Full build guide with the n8n JSON](/drops/automate)

### 8. Every inbound lead auto-logged + tagged

**Saves ~2 hrs/week · Easy · Zapier or n8n + Airtable/Notion**

Whenever someone fills a form, DMs you, or emails in interested, it lands in one
CRM row automatically (name, contact, source, date), so you know which channel
is actually working without checking four inboxes.

**Trigger → action:** new form, email or DM → Zapier parses the source → adds a
tagged row to your Airtable/Notion CRM.

**Setup:** add a *source* field to your CRM first, then point every intake
channel at the same automation so nothing lands untracked.
→ [Full build guide with the n8n JSON](/drops/automate)

### 9. "Gone quiet" lead nudge

**Saves deals that would've quietly died · Easy · Notion/Airtable CRM + scheduled reminder**

If a lead or client hasn't replied in 5 days you get a nudge to follow up, so
nobody falls through the cracks just because your week got busy.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled daily check → filter CRM rows where *last
contacted* is older than 5 days and status isn't closed → send yourself a
reminder.

**Setup:** this needs a *last contacted* date field you actually keep current.
The automation is only as good as that one field; update it right after each
touchpoint.

### 10. Spreadsheet row → draft proposal/invoice

**Saves ~30 min per proposal · Medium · Zapier + Google Docs or PandaDoc**

Fill in one row with a client's info and scope, and it generates a formatted
proposal or invoice from your template, with no retyping the same document structure
every time.

**Trigger → action:** new or updated Google Sheets row → Zapier maps fields into
a Docs/PandaDoc template → generates the finished document.

**Setup:** build the template with merge fields matching your column names
*exactly*. Mismatched field names are the single most common reason this breaks.

## Admin and inbox

*Automations that get the boring, repeated tasks off your plate entirely.*

### 11. 6pm AI-summarized inbox digest

**Saves ~5 hrs/week · Medium · n8n + Gmail + Claude/GPT**

At a fixed time every evening, an automation reads everything that landed in
your inbox that day and sends a short summary, so you check it in 30 seconds
instead of scrolling.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled daily at 6pm → pull that day's Gmail threads →
summarize via Claude/GPT → send to Slack or email.

**Setup:** exclude newsletters and promotions from the pull first, or the
summary gets diluted with noise you didn't need summarized.

### 12. Complaint/bug email → auto task

**Saves ~1 hr/week, and nothing gets buried · Easy · Zapier + Gmail + Linear/Trello/Notion**

When someone emails a complaint or a bug report it automatically becomes a task
on your board, so it can't get lost in your inbox.

**Trigger → action:** new Gmail matching a label or filter → Zapier creates a
task in Linear/Trello/Notion with the email body attached.

**Setup:** set up a Gmail filter that auto-labels support-type email first. That
label is the trigger; otherwise it fires on every email you get.

### 13. Booking form → calendar + reminder

**Saves ~1.5 hrs/week · Easy · Calendly (free tier), or Zapier + Google Calendar**

Someone books a call and it automatically creates the calendar event and sends
both of you the invite, with no back-and-forth finding a time.

**Trigger → action:** new Calendly booking (native), or with a plain form: new
submission → Zapier → create Google Calendar event → send invite.

**Setup:** if you're not tied to a custom form, Calendly does this end-to-end
with zero extra tools. Easiest one on this whole list to turn on.

### 14. Voice memo → transcribed, organized note

**Saves ~2 hrs/week · Medium · n8n or Zapier + transcription API + Notion/Docs**

Record a voice memo while walking and it's automatically transcribed into your
notes app, good for catching ideas before they disappear.

**Trigger → action:** new voice memo file → transcription API → create a page in
Notion or Google Docs with the transcript.

**Setup:** point your phone's voice-memo app at a synced folder (iCloud, Drive)
so new recordings trigger it automatically, with no manual export step.

### 15. Nightly automatic project backup

**Saves your worst possible day · Easy · n8n or Zapier + Google Drive/Dropbox**

Every night your project files, code or documents get copied to a backup folder
automatically, so a crash or a bad overwrite never costs more than a day.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled nightly → sync or copy the watched folder → a
timestamped backup folder.

**Setup:** keep backups timestamped by date rather than overwriting one folder,
so you can roll back further than just "last night" when you catch a mistake
late.

## Personal ops and knowledge

*Automations for keeping your own head clear, not just your business.*

### 16. One morning brief: calendar + weather + tasks

**Saves ~2 hrs/week · Medium · n8n + calendar/weather API + task tool**

Every morning one message lands with your calendar, the weather and your top 3
tasks, so the day starts in one place instead of five app-switches before
coffee.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled daily at 7am → pull calendar + weather + top
tasks → combine into one digest → send to your phone.

**Setup:** cap it at 3 tasks even if your list is longer. The point is a fast
glance, not a full to-do dump.

### 17. Screenshots auto-sorted into dated folders

**Saves ~30 min/week · Easy · Apple Shortcuts (Mac) or Power Automate (Windows)**

Instead of screenshots piling up on your desktop, they sort into dated folders
the moment you take them.

**Trigger → action:** new file in the Screenshots folder → move to a folder
named by today's date, creating it if it doesn't exist.

**Setup:** free and native on both Mac and Windows, with no third-party account,
just a Shortcut or Flow watching one folder.

### 18. Weekly contact/spreadsheet dedupe

**Saves ~1 hr/week, and keeps your data usable · Medium · n8n scheduled dedupe**

Once a week an automation scans your contact list or spreadsheet and flags (or
removes) duplicate entries, so your data doesn't quietly turn into a mess over
months.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled weekly → scan rows for matching email or name →
flag duplicates in a review column, or auto-merge if you trust it.

**Setup:** start with *flag, don't delete* for the first few runs until you
trust the matching logic. Switch to auto-remove once it's proven accurate.

### 19. Website uptime check + instant alert

**Protects revenue, not just time · Easy · UptimeRobot (free tier) + Slack/SMS**

Every few minutes something pings your website. If it's down you get a text or
Slack message immediately, instead of finding out from an angry customer.

**Trigger → action:** scheduled ping every 5–10 min → site fails to respond →
instant Slack or SMS alert.

**Setup:** UptimeRobot's free tier covers this natively, with no Zapier or n8n
required, just connect Slack or your phone number and pick a check interval.

### 20. Negative comment/review → flagged for reply

**Saves ~1 hr/week, and protects your reputation · Medium · n8n + AI sentiment + Slack**

Instead of scrolling for bad feedback, an automation reads new comments and
reviews and flags the negative ones, so you can reply fast before it snowballs.

**Trigger → action:** new comment or review → AI sentiment check → if negative,
send a Slack alert with the comment text and link.

**Setup:** tune the sentiment threshold conservatively at first. Better a few
false positives than a missed real complaint.
